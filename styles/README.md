# Style cards

Directions a film can take. The brief offers three or four as a line-up; the concept panel can also
invent its own. Add a card whenever a reference teaches something new (`motion-reference` skill:
`python studio/tools/breakdown.py <clip>` measures it). Pace is the fastest tenth of the picture,
median, in % of the frame width per second; "still" is the share of time under 2.

## A. Continuous brand reel

- One continuous camera move, no cuts: every hand-off is an object becoming the next thing.
- Brand-exact: official marks, the product's real UI redrawn from its code, tokens copied verbatim.
  If the brand has no mascot, the mark itself comes to life and returns to the exact logo.
- 30 s on a bar grid (120 or 128 BPM), one idea per beat. Pace 0.2-3.7, still 36-75 %.
- A score synthesized from the cue list, with a signature sound per brand.

## B. Motion-designer showreel

- A montage of disciplines: kinetic type (heavy grotesk with an italic serif), a 3D voxel wave, data
  viz, particle streaks, a product UI card, liquid blobs, an end card; a HUD frame with mono labels.
- A cut every ~1.7 s; 0.2 s smears between still holds. Pace 9, still 30 %. Loud, flat master.

## C. Product ad with a halftone mascot

- White, one blue, black type; a halftone-shaded 3D mascot cutout moving between UI moments;
  italic serif accents inside grotesk headlines. Calm: three cuts in 15 s. Pace 4, still 31 %.

## D. SaaS launch in glass and gradient

- Soft gradients on white, glassy cards, light large type with one word in a gradient; a prompt typed
  live, cursor clicks, stacking notifications, agents thinking then ready, a counter. Pace 7.

## E. Match-cut montage

- Every shot shares one shape at one place in frame (a horizon, a circle); a cut every half beat;
  words ride the shape and change every few shots; a quiet logo at the end.

## F. Riso-print anime

- Three inks on cream paper, halftone shading, misregistration; one generated character throughout;
  huge condensed words slammed per lyric; running devices (a meter, a date stamp, underlined captions).
- 150 shots in 2:20, mostly half-beat cuts. Pace 14.

## G. 90s Japanese PC game

- Dithered 16-colour art, CRT scanlines, a boot screen, visual-novel dialogue boxes, status screens,
  pixel skies; glowing chrome logotype words. 190 shots in 2:37. Pace 11.

## H. Watercolor cartoon

- Flat cartoon characters on watercolor paper, theatre curtains, pastel rooms, boxed subtitles.
  About 2 s a shot, gentle motion.

## I. Dark kinetic lines

- Black with one hot accent and cream; line art, contour fields, wireframe 3D rooms, giant mono
  numbers, a prompt typed live, type on paths, paper forms with stamps. Nearly all procedural.
  Long single moves mixed with half-beat cuts. Pace 12.
